Refund policy: Customers can request a full refund within 30 days of purchase.
After 30 days, refunds are evaluated case by case and may be issued as store
credit. Digital downloads are refundable only if the file was not accessed.

Warranty: All hardware products carry a 12-month limited warranty covering
manufacturing defects. The warranty does not cover accidental damage, misuse,
or unauthorized repairs. Extended coverage up to 36 months is available at
checkout for an additional fee.

Shipping: We ship to 40 countries. Standard shipping takes 5-10 business days;
express shipping takes 2-4 business days. Shipping costs are calculated at
checkout based on destination and package weight. Orders over $75 ship free
within the continental US.

Support: Our support team answers within one business day via the help desk.
For urgent production incidents, premium-plan customers can use the priority
channel with a 4-hour response SLA.

Accounts: Each account supports up to 5 team members on the starter plan and
unlimited members on the business plan. Admins can invite, suspend, or remove
members from the settings page. Two-factor authentication is available for all
plans and required on the enterprise plan.
